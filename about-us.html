About Our Company

Flavoured Dry Fruits Manufacturer

RK Dry Fruit is a trusted name in the dry fruits and healthy snacks industry, offering premium quality products with natural freshness, rich nutrition, and great taste. Our range includes almonds, cashews, walnuts, pistachios, raisins, and healthy seeds, carefully sourced to ensure purity and superior quality.

Based in India, we focus on hygienic processing, quality control, and premium packaging to preserve freshness and nutritional value. Every product is handled with care to deliver the best taste and health benefits to our customers.

Our Story

The RK Dry Fruits Journey

From a small family dream to one of India's leading flavoured dry fruits brands — our journey is built on passion, quality, and relentless innovation.

The Beginning

Founded with a Vision

RK Dry Fruits began as a small family venture in Sampla, Haryana, with a simple dream — to deliver the purest and freshest dry fruits to Indian households. Starting with just traditional almonds and cashews, the foundation was laid with passion and integrity.

Innovation Era

Flavoured Revolution

Recognizing a gap in the market, RK Dry Fruits pioneered the concept of gourmet flavoured dry fruits — introducing unique varieties like Badam Rose, Kaju Paan, Chocodip, and many more. This innovation set us apart from competitors and captured hearts nationwide.

Expansion

Pan-India Presence

With growing demand, RK Dry Fruits expanded its distribution network across major cities including Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur, Chandigarh, and many more. Our products reached retail stores, supermarkets, and gifting markets across the country.

Today & Beyond

Leading with 100+ Products

Today, RK Dry Fruits boasts a catalogue of 100+ gourmet products — from traditional Badam Silver to innovative Chocodip and Peri Peri varieties. We continue to innovate, ensuring every customer enjoys the perfect blend of health and flavour.
